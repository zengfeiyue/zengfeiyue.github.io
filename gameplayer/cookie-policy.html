What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

什么是 Cookie 和类似技术？

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. GamePlay is a web-based tool. It uses browser local storage (localStorage) rather than traditional tracking cookies to save your test results and ladder ranking entries. This policy refers to all of these technologies collectively as "Cookies."

Cookie 是您访问的网站放置在您的计算机或移动设备上的小型文本文件。GamePlay 是一个基于网页的工具。它使用浏览器本地存储（localStorage）而非传统的跟踪 Cookie 来保存您的测试成绩和天梯排名条目。本政策将所有这些技术统称为"Cookie"。

How We Use Cookies

我们如何使用 Cookie

We use local storage technologies for the following essential purposes:

我们将本地存储技术用于以下基本目的：

  • To operate our Services: Some local storage is strictly necessary to allow us to operate the tool, primarily to store your test settings and scores.
    运营我们的服务：某些本地存储对于我们运营该工具是绝对必要的，主要用于存储您的测试设置和成绩。
  • To store your preferences and scores: We use local storage to remember your best score, test history, and ladder ranking entries on your device, so you don't lose them between visits.
    存储您的偏好和成绩：我们使用本地存储在您的设备上记住您的最佳成绩、测试历史以及天梯排名条目，以便您在不同次访问之间不会丢失它们。

How to Control These Technologies

如何控制这些技术

Because we only use local storage that is essential for the tool's functionality, there is no option to disable it without affecting your ability to save your scores and rankings. The information is stored locally and can be cleared by clearing your browser data for this site, or by using the "清空本地榜单" button on the ladder panel.

由于我们仅使用对该工具功能至关重要的本地存储，因此无法在不影响您保存成绩和排名的情况下禁用它们。这些信息存储在本地，可以通过清除浏览器中本站点的数据，或使用天梯面板上的"清空本地榜单"按钮来清除。
