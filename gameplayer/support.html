GamePlay App Support / GamePlay 支持与帮助

Welcome to the support page for the GamePlay app, an esports reaction speed testing tool that provides reaction speed tests and ladder ranking features.

欢迎来到 GamePlay 的支持页面。GamePlay 是一款用于电竞反应速度测试的工具，提供反应速度测试与天梯排名等功能。

FAQ / 常见问题

Q: How do I start a reaction speed test?

Go to the GamePlay homepage and click the "Start Test" button. As soon as the screen turns green, click the screen as fast as you can to complete one round. A full test consists of 5 rounds, and the average reaction time is used as your final score.

Q: 如何开始一次反应速度测试？
进入 GamePlay 首页，点击「开始测试」按钮。屏幕变为绿色后，以最快速度点击屏幕即可完成一轮。共 5 轮测试，最终取平均反应时间。

Q: What happens if I click before the screen turns green?

Clicking too early will be recorded as a failed round, and that round will be retried automatically. Please wait for the screen to turn green before clicking to get a valid result.

Q: 屏幕变绿前点击了怎么办？
提前点击会被记为一次失败，该轮会自动重试。请务必等待屏幕变绿后再点击，以获得有效成绩。

Q: How do I participate in the ladder ranking?

After completing a test, enter a nickname in the result panel and click "Submit Score". Your score will then be added to the ladder. The ladder is sorted by average reaction time in ascending order — the shorter your time, the higher your rank.

Q: 如何参与天梯排名？
完成测试后，在结果栏输入昵称并点击「提交成绩」，成绩即可进入天梯榜单。榜单根据平均反应时间升序排列，成绩越短排名越靠前。

Q: Where is the ladder ranking data stored?

Currently, ladder ranking data is stored locally in your browser (localStorage) and is only visible to you. A future version will support cloud sync and global leaderboards.

Q: 天梯排名数据存储在哪里？
目前天梯排名数据存储在您的浏览器本地（localStorage），仅您本人可见。后续版本将支持云端同步与全球排行榜。

Q: Why is my result showing as failed?

Please make sure you do not click before the screen turns green. Also, close other background programs to reduce device latency, and use a mouse or device with lower input lag for the most accurate results.

Q: 为什么我的成绩显示为失败？
请确保没有在屏幕变绿前点击，同时建议关闭其他后台程序以减少设备延迟，使用鼠标或触控延迟较低的设备进行测试。
