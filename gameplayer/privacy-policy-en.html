1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Personal information you disclose to us

In Short: We collect personal information that you provide to us.

The personal information that we collect depends on the context of your interactions with us and the Services, the choices you make, and the products and features you use. The personal information we collect may include the following:

  • Player code and country/region flag. When you first use the app, you may set a player code (a nickname/alias) and choose a country or region. These are used to identify your entries on the global leaderboard and are visible to other players.

Sensitive Information. We do not process sensitive information.

Application Data. If you use our Services, we may collect the following information automatically:

  • Training scores, ratings, and mode details. When you complete training sessions or ladder challenges, we record your scores, ratings, and relevant gameplay details (for example, reaction times or hit rates). These are used for your personal records and for global leaderboard ranking. If you agree, your best scores are uploaded to our server and displayed on the global leaderboard.
  • Anonymous device identifier. We generate a random, anonymous identifier on your device to associate your leaderboard entries with you. It does not contain your name, email address, or other personally identifiable information.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA). Our application integrates an advertising SDK. On iOS, we request your permission through the system App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before accessing the advertising identifier, which is used for personalized advertising and measuring ad performance. If you decline, we do not access it and only non-personalized advertising (if any) may be displayed.
  • Log and Usage Data. Log and usage data is service-related, diagnostic, usage, and performance information our servers automatically collect when you access or use our Services. This log data may include your IP address, device information, and information about your activity in the Services (such as date/time stamps and which features you use), device event information (such as system activity and error reports).

We do not collect your email address, real name, postal address, phone number, precise location, or any payment information.

Information collected from other sources

In Short: We do not collect personal information from third-party sources.

We do not obtain personal information about you from public databases, marketing partners, social media platforms, or data brokers.

5. DO WE USE COOKIES AND OTHER TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES?

In Short: The mobile application itself does not use cookies. We may use advertising identifiers and similar technologies when you grant permission.

6. HOW DO WE HANDLE UPLOADING SCORES TO THE GLOBAL LEADERBOARD?

In Short: We obtain your consent before uploading any score to the global leaderboard, and you can change your decision at any time.

Before your score is uploaded to our server for the global leaderboard, the app shows you a consent dialog that clearly explains that your player code, country/region flag, and best score will be displayed on the global leaderboard and visible to other players. You can choose "Agree & Upload" or "Save Locally Only". If you decline, your score is stored only on your device and is never uploaded.

You can change your decision at any time via the switch in the app (Me → Settings → Upload to Global Leaderboard). Turning the switch off stops any further uploads.

12. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ("DNT") feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognizing and implementing DNT signals has been finalized. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognizing or honoring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time. On iOS, we honor your tracking preference through the App Tracking Transparency prompt, which is shown before any advertising identifier is accessed.
