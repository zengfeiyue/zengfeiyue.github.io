1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They were originally used so that a website could recognize a user's device. They are widely used to make websites work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

Cookies set by the website owner (in this case, Lai Qiao(赖巧)) are called "first-party cookies." Cookies set by parties other than the website owner are called "third-party cookies."

2. WHAT ARE COOKIES USED FOR?

First-party cookies are necessary for our website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you, such as setting privacy preferences or filling in forms. Third-party cookies are used to report on usage and performance of our website.

3. WHAT TYPES OF COOKIES DO WE USE?

We use the following categories of cookies on our website:

Cookie Types Source Purpose
Required cookies First-party Used for technical purposes essential to operate our website, such as remembering your cookie preferences. These cannot be disabled.
Analytics cookies Third-party (e.g., Google Analytics) Help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously, such as page views and referral sources.
Advertising / Marketing cookies Third-party (e.g., ad network partners) Used to deliver and measure advertisements shown on our free web properties, where applicable.

Our mobile application uses device identifiers (rather than browser cookies) solely for anonymous analytics and crash reporting. These identifiers are not linked to your voice or translated text.

4. HOW CAN YOU CONTROL COOKIES?

You can decide whether to accept or reject cookies through your browser controls. Most browsers allow you to manage cookies in their settings. To learn more about how to control cookies, see www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our website, though some basic features may be affected. Within our mobile application, you can limit tracking by adjusting your device's advertising and privacy settings.
